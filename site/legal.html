About Hypewise

Hypewise tracks US resale prices of popular sneakers and shows how each price has moved over time, so buyers and resellers can see the market at a glance. We focus on sneakers today and plan to add more categories later.

Questions, ideas, partnerships or press: write to hypewise.team@gmail.com. You can also find us on X at @gethypewise. Reddit, Instagram and TikTok accounts are coming soon.

Disclaimer

Prices are market estimates in US dollars, calculated from StockX data supplied through KicksDB. They can differ from the actual price when you buy or sell. Privacy

Hypewise does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal data such as your name or email. We do not run advertising trackers. We count visits with a simple tool of our own: when a page opens we store the page address, the website you came from (for example X), your language, your screen width and a random code that lasts only for that browser tab session. We do not store your IP address, do not use cookies, do not build profiles and do not follow you across other sites. Only totals are ever shown. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages. Price data is read from our database; nothing about you is written to it.

If we add accounts or alerts in the future, we will update this page before doing so.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use cookies. The random visit code in our counter lives only in the tab's session storage and disappears when you close the tab. Your browser's local storage keeps two small preferences on your own device: your chosen theme and language. A session setting remembers if you closed the top banner. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.
