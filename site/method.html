How the signal works

Beta · English only for now · Last updated: November 2, 2026

What it is

For each sneaker, Hypewise looks at its daily average US resale price history and shows one of three labels: Cop (the price is low compared with its own recent history), Wait (no clear edge) or Sell (the price is high compared with its own recent history). It is a way to read a price chart quickly. It does not predict the future.

The five factors

Each factor is scored from -1 (sell) to +1 (cop) and combined into a weighted score from -100 to +100.

  • Position in range (30%): where today's price sits between the lowest and highest price seen.
  • Distance from average (20%): how far today's price is from the typical price, measured in standard deviations (z-score).
  • Short-term momentum (20%): a fast moving average (about 3 days) compared with a slower one (about 10 days).
  • 21-day trend (15%): the slope of a straight line through the last 21 days, weighted by how well the line fits.
  • Distance from the peak (15%): how far the price has fallen from its highest point.

Safeguards

  • Outlier cleaning: readings that jump more than about 35% away from their neighbours are ignored, because they are usually data glitches.
  • Calm data counts more: very volatile prices weaken the verdict, and prices that barely move are pulled toward Wait.
  • No catching falling knives: if the price is still dropping fast, Hypewise will not say Cop, even if it looks cheap.
  • Enough data: a signal needs at least 8 readings over at least 7 days. Otherwise you will see no signal.
  • Thresholds: Cop at a score of +30 or more, Sell at -30 or less, Wait in between.

Confidence

The confidence figure goes up with more readings over more days, with calmer prices and when the five factors agree. It never goes above 85%, because no model of resale prices deserves more.

Limits you should know

  • It is unproven. Hypewise only has a few weeks of daily data. We have not yet shown that the signal predicts real price moves, and we will publish how it performs once there is enough history.
  • Prices are averages from market data, not a price you can necessarily buy or sell at. Fees, shipping, size and condition change what you actually pay or receive.
  • News, releases, restocks and trends can move prices overnight and are not in the model.
  • Everything on Hypewise is an estimate for information only and not financial advice. Hypewise is not affiliated with any brand or marketplace.
